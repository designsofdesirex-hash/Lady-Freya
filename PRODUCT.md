- Services include customs and worn items.
- Supplied platforms are Telegram, X, Instagram, OnlyFans, MyFeetish, TipFunder, and Throne.
- The site must include an 18+ gate, responsive behavior, accessible controls, and a clear media preview.

## Brand Commitments

Use Lady Freya as the client name. The voice is warm, confident, intimate, teasing, and grounded in luxury, texture, personal attention, and clear consent. Keep adult content clearly marked and consensual in framing.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied Freya image library was optimized into `assets/media/freya/`. No verified pricing sheet or availability details were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Authenticity before theatre.
- Direct contact over cluttered funnels.
- Let Freya's own voice lead.
- Make the offer legible in seconds.
- Treat adult visitors with clear boundaries and respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must retain keyboard focus visibility, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, labelled media controls, and a usable bilingual switch on narrow screens.
