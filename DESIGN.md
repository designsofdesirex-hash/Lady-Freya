# Lady Freya visual system

## Direction

An image-led, warm editorial for Lady Freya, a German luxury feet creator. The design earns its identity through supplied, real-world sole photography, dark walnut surfaces, brushed-gold action colour, and a direct network of official platforms. It contains no booking funnel or generic marketplace language.

## Palette and type

Ink `#171412` is the main evening surface; cream `#f3eee7` creates the quiet editorial pause; sand `#ded3c2` carries the gallery; warm gold `#d7a15d` marks action; rust `#9d5845` adds depth. DM Serif Display is the display face; Manrope handles navigation, body copy, and controls.

## Composition

The first screen is an oversized promise paired with a tall warm-sole portrait. The manifesto is a generous cream pause with an understated EU 38 stamp. Services are two large open rows, not cards. Gallery uses an asymmetrical editorial grid. Contact closes with Telegram and Instagram as the primary doors, followed by the complete supplied platform list.

## Interaction and responsive rules

Buttons are substantial, full-width on small screens, and respond with a short lift and press scale. Image hover uses a restrained scale settle. Reduced motion removes transforms. The desktop hero is two columns; mobile becomes a clean vertical reading path.

The contact close has no form or booking system. It uses one active Instagram action and two non-interactive coming-soon states.

## Content boundaries

English is the only published language. The supplied Freya image set is used and optimized in `assets/media/freya/`. No prices, testimonials, availability, or unsupported claims are fabricated.
